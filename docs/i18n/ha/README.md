**Buɗe fayilolin maƙunsar bayanai na kwamfutarka kai tsaye a Google Sheets daga Windows.**

ZagoSheetsWin ƙaramin manhajar Windows ce da ke sauƙaƙa buɗe maƙunsar bayanai na cikin kwamfuta:

**danna fayil sau biyu → ɗora shi da canza tsari → buɗe a Google Sheets**

Bayan an shigo da fayil cikin nasara, ZagoSheetsWin na iya maye gurbin asalin fayil ɗin da gajeriyar hanyar Intanet (`.url`) zuwa takardar Google Sheets. Haka kuma yana ajiye kwafin asalin fayil a kwamfutar da za a iya dawo da shi.

Manufar ita ce Google Sheets ya kasance kamar manhajar Windows ta yau da kullum wajen buɗe fayilolin maƙunsar bayanai.

## Abin da yake yi

ZagoSheetsWin yana haɗa fayilolin maƙunsar bayanai na Windows da Google Sheets. Lokacin da ka buɗe fayil da ake tallafawa, manhajar na iya:

- gane fayil ɗin da tabbatar da ingancinsa;
- ƙirƙirar kwafin ajiya da za a iya dawo da shi;
- ɗora fayil kai tsaye zuwa Google Drive ta API na hukuma na Google;
- canza shi zuwa ainihin tsarin takardar Google Sheets;
- buɗe sakamakon a burauzar da aka saita;
- ƙirƙirar gajeriyar hanyar `.url` zuwa takardar Google;
- kauce wa sake ɗora fayil ɗaya idan an sake buɗe shi.

Ba sai an ɗora fayil da hannu zuwa Drive ko an nemi shi a burauza ko an sake canza shi a kowane lokaci ba.

## Nau'ikan fayil da ake tallafawa

Nau'ikan da ake ƙoƙarin tallafawa yanzu su ne `.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.ods`, `.csv`, da `.tsv`.

Wasu nau'ikan na iya samun ƙarin iyakokin dacewa. Fayilolin da ke da abubuwan da ba za a iya kiyaye su lafiya ba ana sarrafa su da taka-tsantsan don kauce wa ɓacewar bayanai ba tare da gargaɗi ba.

## An tsara shi don Windows

An gina ZagoSheetsWin musamman don Windows. Ya haɗa da **Open with (Buɗe da)**, rajistar nau'in fayil, buɗewa da danna sau biyu, haɗin File Explorer na zaɓi da manhajar girka ta Windows.

Manhajar ba ta canza tsoffin manhajojin Windows a ɓoye. Mai amfani ne ke sarrafa haɗin fayil da manhaja.

## Tsaro tun daga ƙira

Sauya fayil na kwamfuta aiki ne da ya kamata a iya juyawa. Kafin cire asalin fayil daga babban fayil ɗinsa, manhajar tana tabbatar da cewa:

1. akwai kwafin ajiya da za a iya dawo da shi;
2. an ƙirƙiri takardar Google Sheets cikin nasara;
3. an ajiye bayanan haɗin fayil na dindindin;
4. an rubuta gajeriyar hanyar Intanet kuma an tantance ta.

Idan aiki ya gaza, asalin fayil yana nan lafiya.

> Kada a taɓa lalata bayanan mai amfani a ɓoye.

## Kwafin ajiya

Ana iya ajiye asalin fayil a wurin kwafin ajiya na sirri a kwamfuta kafin a sauya shi da gajeriyar hanya. Ana iya saita iyakar sararin ajiya, tsawon lokacin riƙewa da zaɓuɓɓukan tsaftacewa.

Kwafin ajiya yana kare asalin fayil ɗin da aka shigo da shi. **Ba tsarin daidaita bayanai ta hanyoyi biyu ba ne**: canje-canjen da aka yi daga baya a Google Sheets ba sa komawa cikin asalin fayil.

## Sirri da damar shiga Google

ZagoSheetsWin yana tuntuɓar API na Google kai tsaye daga kwamfutarka.

- Ba a aika abubuwan da ke cikin fayilolin ka zuwa sabobin Zagotools.
- Ana ajiye alamomin OAuth a kwamfuta tare da kariyar tsaron Windows.
- Izinin `drive.file` yana iyakance damar shiga fayilolin da aka ƙirƙira ko aka buɗe ta wannan manhajar.
- Shigo da fayil ba ya buƙatar bin diddigin nazari.

Manufar Sirri da Sharuɗɗan Amfani: https://zagotools.top/legal.html

## Matsayin aikin

Ana ci gaba da haɓaka ZagoSheetsWin, kuma a yanzu **manhaja ce ta matakin alpha**. Babban tsarin Windows → Google Sheets yana aiki, yayin da ake inganta girkawa, maido da bayanai, dacewar nau'ikan fayil, tallafin harsuna da sauƙin amfani. Ana iya samun canje-canje kafin fitowar sigar farko tabbatacciya.

## Dangantakarsa da Open in Google

ZagoSheetsWin ya samo asali daga [Open in Google](https://github.com/SwatiK425/open-in-google) na [SwatiK425](https://github.com/SwatiK425), wanda ya ba shi tushe da ra'ayi na farko.

Yanzu ZagoSheetsWin manhajar Windows ce mai zaman kanta, da nata tsarin gini, mai girkawa, fuskar amfani, ajiyar bayanai da maido da su, haɗin fayil, sarrafa tsare-tsaren fayil da hanyar buɗe fayil na kwamfuta a Google Sheets. Asalin aikin yana ci gaba da kasancewa mai zaman kansa; za a iya ba da shawarar ingantawa na gama-gari ga upstream idan ya dace.

## Buɗaɗɗen tushe

ZagoSheetsWin manhaja ce kyauta mai buɗaɗɗen tushe. Tana kiyaye sunan marubucin da sharuddan lasisin asalin Open in Google, tare da bayyana sabbin ayyukan ZagoSheetsWin / Zagotools.
